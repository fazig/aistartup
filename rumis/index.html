Our Story

Crafted with Love,
Served with Soul

Born from a passion for the perfect brew, Rumi's is more than a coffee house — it's a sanctuary. Named after the great poet Rumi, we believe every cup should inspire, comfort, and awaken the senses.

Our beans are ethically sourced from the highlands of Colombia, Ethiopia, and Guatemala, roasted in small batches to preserve their unique character.
